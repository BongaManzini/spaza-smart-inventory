Informatics Value for the Informal Economy

South African Spaza shop owners lose an estimated 18% of their monthly revenue by running out of stock of basic staples (maize meal, cooking oil, bread) between the 1st and 5th of the month. Traditional ERP software is prohibitively expensive and complicated. Spaza-Smart demonstrates how lean algorithmic heuristics running on basic mobile web or USSD empower micro-entrepreneurs with predictive purchasing power.
